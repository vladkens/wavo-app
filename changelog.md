## v0.1.0 – 2026-10-31

First release: local dictation for macOS, Windows and Linux. Hold a hotkey, speak, and the text lands where you were typing.

### Features

- Added dictation into any app: hold the hotkey and speak, or tap it to start and stop.
- Added speech recognition on your computer in about 100 languages, on the GPU or a Mac's Neural Engine: no account, and your voice never leaves the machine.
- Added the menu bar and tray app, with a small on-screen pill that shows your voice while you speak.
- Added automatic microphone choice: a USB microphone first, a Bluetooth headset only when there is no other.
- Added the history of your dictations: search, play back, copy, or transcribe again with another model, and bring in your history from Handy.
- Added updates in place: Wavo checks once a day and installs a new version when you choose.
- Added downloads for Apple Silicon Macs (signed and notarized), Windows on x64 and ARM64, and Linux as AppImage and .deb.
