# File transcripts

Goal: the person drops an audio or video file onto the settings window, or picks one with "Transcribe File…", and gets its text with times in a Files tab. It is kept until they delete it, and they can read it there, copy it, and export it to TXT and SRT. A 4.5-hour lecture recorded on a phone works:
- memory stays small;
- the tab shows progress and time left;
- a dictation still goes first;
- a quit or a crash loses at most a minute of work.

Dictation history, its limits and "Until Wavo quits" don't change. Meetings come later on the same tables and viewer.

Why: it is the most-upvoted request among competitors (Handy discussion #299, 61 votes), and the engine needs nothing new for it. The real case is a student who records 4.5-hour lectures on a phone and transcribes the file afterwards.

## Decisions

- **Three separate things.**
  - Dictations are `entries`; file transcripts come now; meetings come later.
  - A transcript is not a history entry. Retention, limits, the history's Delete All and its search don't touch it. It stays until the person deletes it.
- **Tables in `history.db`, added in `migrate_v2`:**
  - `transcripts`:
    - `id`, `kind` (`file`; `meeting` later), `created_at`
    - `name`, `path`, `size`, `modified`: the person's file, which is never copied
    - `duration_ms`: NULL until known
    - `model`, `language`: what it ran with; NULL language means auto
    - `state`: `queued`, `running`, `stopped`, `done` or `failed`
    - `done_ms`, `error`
  - `segments`:
    - `id`, `transcript_id`, `start_ms`, `end_ms`, `text`
    - `speaker`: NULL; diarization and a meeting's two tracks fill it later
    - Index on `(transcript_id, start_ms)`.
  - The rows of a transcript and its segments go in one transaction.
  - wavo's `Transcript::utterances(turns)`, in its diarization branch, gives speaker, start, end and text, the same shape as a segment row. Nothing here waits for it.
- **Schema 2 and older versions.** Wavo 0.3.0 refuses a newer schema, so after this version it can't open `history.db`. An archive exported by the new version doesn't import into 0.3.0 either. That comes with the chosen "same database"; the person kept it (answer 8).
- **The store** is `Transcripts` in `src/transcripts.rs`.
  - It opens its own `history::Db` on the on-disk `home/history.db`, also when dictations are kept in memory. The Files tab says transcripts are kept on disk until deleted.
  - Its revision comes from `Db::changes()`, as the history's does.
- **The person's file is not copied.**
  - Wavo keeps its path, size and modified time. The text stays when the file moves, changes or is deleted.
  - Work that needs the file again (going on after a quit, transcribing it again) first checks the size and modified time. A missing or changed file stops that work with a note and "Locate…" (answer 3).
- **Decoding** is a streaming symphonia decode in `src/decode.rs`.
  - Steps: packets to f32, then mono by averaging, then `audio::Resample` to 16 kHz, a piece at a time.
  - The whole file is never held in memory: 4.5 h of 48 kHz stereo as f32 is 6 GB, and even as 16 kHz mono it is 1 GB.
  - Bounds as in the wavo CLI: a rate of 1–384 kHz and a packet of at most 1 MiB. A packet that fails to decode is skipped.
  - Resuming seeks to a time; when the format can't seek, it decodes and skips.
  - The duration comes from the track's frame count when known. Without it, progress shows the time done without a percent.
  - Formats: answer 1. symphonia is added with `cargo add symphonia --no-default-features --features …`, and the binary's growth is measured against the readme's "about 10 MB".
- **Chunks.**
  - The decoded audio is cut at a pause, with `wavo::split`, into chunks of up to `CHUNK_MS` (60 s). Each chunk is one background engine job.
  - A dictation goes first. A background job that yields starts over after it, so a yield redoes at most one chunk.
  - Each chunk's segments and the new `done_ms` are written in one transaction. A quit or a crash therefore loses at most one chunk, and the next run goes on from `done_ms`.
  - Pauses are not shortened (`Clip::engine_input` isn't used), so the times are the file's.
- **Text with times from the engine.**
  - `SpeechModel::transcribe` returns `Heard { text, segments }`, the segments built from the pieces of `transcribe_long` and their token times. Dictations and previews use only `text`.
  - A segment is one sentence: it ends at `.`, `?`, `!`, `…` or `。？！`. A longer one is cut at a word boundary past 10 s or 84 characters, so a segment is also an SRT cue of two lines of 42.
  - It starts at the time of its first word's token. It ends where the next segment starts; the last segment of a piece ends where the piece ends, which is the next piece's start or the chunk's end.
  - Moonshine has no token times, so it gets one segment per piece with the piece's times. Whisper's tokens carry their segment's start, so it gets one segment per Whisper segment.
- **One file at a time**, in the order added; the rest wait. While one runs:
  - Progress is `done_ms / duration_ms`, plus the pieces of the chunk in progress from the engine's status.
  - Time left comes from this run's speed.
  - `nimbo::activity::begin` and user-initiated QoS are held, as an export does, so App Nap doesn't slow the work and the Mac doesn't sleep when idle (answer 5).
- **Model and language.**
  - Files have their own `files_model` and `files_language` settings (answer 4).
  - The files work holds a clone of the dictation's `Engine` when the model file is the same, and otherwise starts its own. That engine ends when the queue is empty.
  - Its own engine gets no chunk while a dictation records or is transcribed.
  - The language list is the dictation's without "Keyboard layout".
- **The viewer** is `src/viewer.rs`, a module of its own so that meetings reuse it.
  - Segments are grouped into paragraphs: a new one after a pause of 2 s or more, or after 6 segments. Each paragraph shows its start time.
  - It is a `virtual_col` with heights from `measure_wrapped`, and its text can be selected.
  - A transcript still running grows at the end without moving the scroll.
  - v1 has no editing and no playback from a time (answer 7).
- **Export and copy.**
  - TXT has the viewer's paragraphs without times. SRT has one cue per segment.
  - Both go through `FileDialog::save`, named after the file. Copy All copies the TXT text.
- **Errors** are shown in plain words in the tab: a format Wavo can't read, a file not found or changed, a model that failed.
  - A failed chunk fails the transcript. The text done so far stays, and "Try Again" goes on from `done_ms`.
- **Delivery.**
  - One PR from `feat/files`; a nimbo PR only if a missing piece turns up (answer 9).
  - `next/fixes` is not touched.

## Answers

The person took the recommendations, 2026-11-15:

1. Formats: wav, mp3, m4a/aac, alac, mp4/mov, flac, ogg (Vorbis), aiff, caf, mkv/webm without Opus. An Opus file gets a plain error.
2. Files tab after History. The tray menu gets "Transcribe File…" (it opens the tab and the picker) and a progress line while a file runs. A drop on any tab switches the window to Files.
3. The text stays. When the file is needed again and is gone or changed, the transcript says so and offers "Locate…".
4. Own Model and Language on the Files tab: "Same as Dictation" (the `model` setting, not a keyboard layout's) and Auto by default.
5. One file at a time, a dictation first. Stop keeps the text and Continue goes on from there. An unfinished one resumes by itself at the next launch, and the Mac is kept awake while a file runs.
6. Transcribe Again makes a new transcript beside the old one.
7. Paragraphs with times, selection, TXT without times, SRT with times. v1 has no editing, no playback from a time and no search inside a transcript; the list's search covers names and text.
8. The history archive leaves file transcripts out. Schema 2 in `history.db` stays, and 0.3.0 can't open it afterwards.
9. The picker takes one file and a drop takes many; nimbo isn't changed.

### 2. Decoding

Files: new `src/decode.rs`, `src/audio.rs` (`Resample` made `pub(crate)`), `Cargo.toml` (through `cargo add`), `scripts/fixtures.py`, `Makefile` (`MODEL_TESTS`).

- [ ] `Decoder::open(path)` probes the file by its extension and content and takes the first track with audio. It gives the duration when known and the codec's name.
- [ ] `Decoder::next()` gives the next part of 16 kHz mono, `None` at the end. `Decoder::seek(ms)` goes to a time.
- [ ] Clear errors, each tested:
  - not audio, or a format Wavo can't read;
  - a video without sound;
  - an Opus track ("Opus isn't supported yet");
  - an empty file;
  - a rate or packet out of bounds.
- [ ] The chunker cuts the stream at pauses with `wavo::split` into chunks of up to `CHUNK_MS` and keeps the rest for the next chunk. No sample is lost or repeated.
- [ ] Tests on WAVs generated into a `TempDir` (44.1 kHz stereo, 8 kHz mono, 24-bit, float):
  - the length and a tone's frequency are kept;
  - a seek matches decoding and skipping to within 10 ms;
  - a 30-minute file goes through in chunks with no buffer over two chunks.
- [ ] `scripts/fixtures.py` makes these with ffmpeg into `target/fixtures`:
  - mp3, m4a (AAC), flac, ogg and an mp4 video of a speech fixture;
  - a long file of about 4.5 h made of fixtures and silence, as a 64 kbps m4a.
- [ ] A real-model test in `MODEL_TESTS` checks that each format gives the WAV's text.

### 4. The files work

Files: new `src/files.rs`, `src/session.rs`, `src/settings.rs`, `src/app.rs`.

- [ ] New requests:
  - `Request::Transcribe(Vec<PathBuf>)`;
  - `StopFile(id)`, `ContinueFile(id)`;
  - `DeleteFiles(ids)`;
  - `TranscribeAgain { id, model, language }`;
  - `LocateFile(id, path)`.
- [ ] The queue runs one file at a time.
  - A decoder thread works one chunk ahead through a bounded channel.
  - Chunks go to the engine as background jobs. On `Done` the chunk is appended and the next one goes.
  - Stop cancels the job and keeps the text done so far. Delete while running cancels first.
- [ ] Engine choice and settings as in the decisions and answer 4: `files_model` and `files_language` in `Settings`, with defaults.
- [ ] At start, unfinished transcripts resume (answer 5) when the file is the same. Otherwise they stop with "file changed" or "file not found".
- [ ] Progress reaches the window:
  - `Status::files` gives the running transcript with `done_ms`, `duration_ms`, the time left and the queue length;
  - `Update::transcripts` is a revision, so the window reloads;
  - the activity is held while a file runs.
- [ ] Quitting, by `Request::Close` or by dropping the session, drops the chunk in progress. The state stays `running` for the next start.
- [ ] Tests with `FakeModels` on WAVs generated under a temp `WAVO_HOME`:
  - a whole run;
  - a dictation in the middle of a file goes first, and the file goes on;
  - resume after the session is dropped mid-file: it goes on from `done_ms` and no segment appears twice;
  - stop and continue;
  - delete while running;
  - a moved file and a changed file;
  - a failed chunk;
  - two files in the queue;
  - with the history in memory, transcripts stay on disk;
  - a files model other than the dictation's uses its own engine, which ends with the queue.

## Checks

Each task runs `make check` and `make test` in this worktree. Tasks 2–4 also run `make test-models` with the models in the Hugging Face cache, read only.

Never open the app or a window, never record from the microphone, and use a temp `WAVO_HOME` in every run.

The long fixture is run once on a real model with peak memory measured by `/usr/bin/time -l`. Memory must stay under 300 MB above the model's.
